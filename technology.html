The Technology Behind Orin

A multi-model AI system engineered for bioscience — combining intelligent routing, scientific databases, and domain-specific reasoning into a unified research platform.

Built to think,
not just respond.

Orin does not rely on a single AI model. Every query is analyzed, classified, and routed through a system designed specifically for bioscience reasoning — ensuring accuracy, efficiency, and contextual understanding.

Query InputUser asks a bioscience question
ClassificationIntent and complexity analyzed
Model RoutingAssigned to optimal AI engine
Data RetrievalReal-time DB grounding
Structured OutputRigorous, formatted insights
01

Intelligent Routing

Every query is first understood — not answered. Orin identifies intent and routes it to the most suitable model, balancing speed, cost, and reasoning depth.

02

Multi-Model Intelligence

Different models handle different tasks. Lightweight queries use fast, cost-efficient models, while complex scientific reasoning is handled by high-capability systems.

03

Scientific Data Layer

Orin connects to real-time scientific databases, ensuring that every answer is grounded in verified research, not static training data.

04

Structured Reasoning

Responses are not just generated — they are structured into clear scientific logic: mechanism, evidence, clinical relevance, and limitations.
